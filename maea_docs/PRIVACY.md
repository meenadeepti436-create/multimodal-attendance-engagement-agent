# Privacy & Ethics Note — MAEA
- Runs fully offline; no cloud calls, no telemetry.
- Raw video/audio are not stored by default; only derived features (scores, timestamps).
- Participants are pseudonymised (S01, S02...) unless they opted into enrolment.
- Face embeddings (if enrolled) are encrypted locally; deletable via `scripts/purge_data.py`.
- No speech-to-text; audio is used only to measure activity levels.
- Engagement scores are indicators for instructors, not for grading, ranking or discipline.
- Obtain informed consent and follow your institution's policy before deployment.
